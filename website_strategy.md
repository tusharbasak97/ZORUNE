## 5. Technical Formulation Standards: Zero-Water Physics

1. **$0.00\%$ Added Water ($0.00\%\text{ H}_2\text{O}$)**:
   - Eliminates phase-separation turbidity and microbial growth risk under extreme tropical heat and humidity.
2. **The Quad Co-Solvent Carrier Matrix**:
   - **IPM (Isopropyl Myristate)**: Lowers surface tension, promotes silky cutaneous spread, and retards volatile evaporation.
   - **TEC (Triethyl Citrate)**: Fixative solvent that anchors top-note citrus aldehydes.
   - **DPG (Dipropylene Glycol)**: Solubilizes heavy natural resins (Benzoin, Labdanum, Orris).
   - **Glucam P-20 (PPG-20 Methyl Glucose Ether)**: Slow-release humectant providing 14–20 hour skin sillage.
3. **60-Day Low-Temperature Nitrogen Maceration**:
   - All bulk compounds aged in nitrogen-sparged 316L stainless steel vats at 12°C for 60 days before cold filtration through 0.22-micron sterile membranes.
