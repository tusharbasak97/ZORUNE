## 1. Research Methodology & Sources

This document compiles ongoing terroir research, cooperative verification data, and olfactive formulation intelligence supporting the 20 sovereign creations in the ZORUNE catalog. Research covers five continental sourcing regions across Japan, Europe, South Africa, South Asia, and the Mediterranean.

### Research Scope
- **Botanical provenance verification** — GPS coordinates, elevation, harvest windows, cooperative certifications
- **Supply chain risk assessment** — Climate vulnerability, geopolitical stability, CITES compliance
- **Olfactive chemistry benchmarks** — GC-MS profiles, irone/santalol/safranal content verification
- **Regulatory landscape monitoring** — IFRA 51st/52nd Amendment updates, EU 2023/1545 allergen disclosure, Japan PMD Act compliance

## 3. Regulatory Research Updates

### 3.1 EU Regulation (EU) 2023/1545 — New Allergen Disclosure
- **Deadline:** July 31, 2026 (now in effect)
- **Impact:** 45 newly identified fragrance allergens require individual declaration at >0.001% in leave-on products
- **ZORUNE Action Required:** Full GC-MS quantification of all 20 formulations against expanded allergen list
- **Key molecules affected:** d-Limonene, Linalool, Citral, Geraniol, Alpha-pinene, Beta-caryophyllene, Camphor, Terpineol

### 3.2 Japan PMD Act — Foreign Manufacturer Accreditation
- **Status:** PMDA accreditation application prepared for Grasse/Milan compounding facility
- **MAH Appointment:** Licensed Japanese Marketing Authorization Holder identified
- **JCIA Nomenclature:** All ingredients standardized against MHLW Positive/Negative lists

### 3.3 IFRA 51st/52nd Amendment — Category 4 Limits
- **Oakmoss atranol/chloroatranol:** <2 ppm strict limit (not applicable — ZORUNE uses Evernyl/Veramoss synthetic substitute)
- **Methyl heptine carbonate:** Restricted; trace monitoring required in violet leaf naturals
- **Citral limits:** Affects bergamot-heavy formulations (ZR-U01, ZR-W03, ZR-W05)
